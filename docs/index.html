Pub watchability — the angle

Every fixture includes a note on whether it's realistically watchable from a pub, based on the kick-off time in your local time. Games that kick off in the afternoon or evening in your timezone are pub-friendly. Games that kick off in the morning in your timezone (because they're in Australia or the West Coast of the US) are watchable if you're up early, or you watch at home and meet up later.

Europe — how Irish teams fit in

Irish teams compete in Europe mainly through the provinces in two ways:

  • United Rugby Championship (URC) — a cross-border professional league that includes Irish provinces alongside Scottish, Welsh, Italian, and South African teams. It's the main day-to-day competition for the provinces. Most URC games are in Irish time (afternoon/evening Irish kick-offs).
  • European club competitions (EPCR) — the European Champions Cup and European Challenge Cup. These are the two EPCR competitions. The top URC teams (and other European league top teams) qualify for the Champions Cup; a wider set qualify for the Challenge Cup. Irish provinces enter when qualified.

Kick-off times in Europe: most European away games kick off in the afternoon in the host country, which is usually morning Irish time (for games in France/England/Italy/Scotland/Wales — a few hours ahead). Some late European kick-offs are early evening Irish time. Pacific / far-ahead games (not relevant to Europe) would be early morning Irish time.

Pub watchability for European games: a typical Champions Cup Saturday game in France kicking off at 18:00 local = about 17:00 Irish time — pub-friendly. A midday game in Cardiff = about 12:00 Irish time — pub-friendly. A late Saturday French game at 20:00 local = about 19:00 Irish time — pub-friendly.

World tournaments

Rugby Championship

Annual Southern Hemisphere round-robin: New Zealand, South Africa, Australia, Argentina. July–August. Evening local kick-offs = early morning Irish time — watch at home, meet later. Northern Hemisphere touring sides in Irish summer fall in Irish afternoon/evening — pub-friendly.

Pacific Nations Cup

Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, and guest teams (Japan). July–August. Evening local kick-offs = early morning Irish time — watch at home, meet later.

Rugby Europe Championship

Georgia, Romania, Spain, Portugal. February–June. Evening local kick-offs = morning Irish time (1–3 hours ahead) — pub-friendly if up early, or home watch.

Rugby World Cup 2027

1 Oct – 13 Nov 2027, Australia. 24 teams (men's). Ireland men drawn in Pool D (Scotland, Uruguay, Portugal). Australia is 11 hours ahead (east coast) — evening Australian kick-off = morning Irish time. Final 13 Nov 2027, 20:00 AEDT = 9:00am Irish time — early morning pub possible.

Women's Rugby World Cup 2027

2027 in Ireland (hosts: Ireland, together with Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland). All Ireland games are in Irish time and pub-friendly. If elsewhere, depends on timezone.

Rugby World Cup Sevens

Every four years. 2025 in Saudi Arabia — evening local time = early morning Irish time.

Sevens World Series

Series of 15-a-side sevens tournaments worldwide. October/November–May. Dublin Sevens stop is pub-friendly. Away stops in Europe are morning Irish time — pub-friendly if up early. Stops in Asia/Americas are early morning Irish time.
