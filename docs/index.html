Pub watchability — the angle

Every fixture includes a note on whether it's realistically watchable from a pub, based on the kick-off time in your local time. Games that kick off in the afternoon or evening in your timezone are pub-friendly. Games that kick off in the morning in your timezone (because they're in Australia or the West Coast of the US) are watchable if you're up early, or you watch at home and meet up later.

Europe — how teams fit in

Teams compete in Europe mainly through the provinces in two ways:

  • United Rugby Championship (URC) — a cross-border professional league that includes provinces from Ireland, Scotland, Wales, Italy, and South Africa. It's the main day-to-day competition for the provinces. Most URC games kick off in local time (afternoon/evening local).
  • European club competitions (EPCR) — the European Champions Cup and European Challenge Cup. These are the two EPCR competitions. The top URC teams (and other European league top teams) qualify for the Champions Cup; a wider set qualify for the Challenge Cup. Provinces enter when qualified.

Kick-off times in Europe: most European away games kick off in the afternoon in the host country, which is usually morning local time (for games in France/England/Italy/Scotland/Wales — a few hours ahead). Some late European kick-offs are early evening local time. Pacific / far-ahead games (not relevant to Europe) would be early morning local time.

Pub watchability for European games: a typical Champions Cup Saturday game in France kicking off at 18:00 local = about 17:00 local time — pub-friendly. A midday game in Cardiff = about 12:00 local time — pub-friendly. A late Saturday French game at 20:00 local = about 19:00 local time — pub-friendly.

World national teams

Every national team that competes in world rugby. Kick-off times shown in your local timezone. Pub watchability assessed relative to your local clock.

World tournaments

Rugby Championship

Annual Southern Hemisphere round-robin: New Zealand, South Africa, Australia, Argentina. July–August. Evening local kick-offs = early morning local time — watch at home, meet later. Northern Hemisphere touring sides in local summer fall in local afternoon/evening — pub-friendly.

Pacific Nations Cup

Fiji, Samoa, Tonga, and guest teams (Japan). July–August. Evening local kick-offs = early morning local time — watch at home, meet later.

Rugby Europe Championship

Georgia, Romania, Spain, Portugal. February–June. Evening local kick-offs = morning local time (1–3 hours ahead) — pub-friendly if up early, or home watch.

Rugby World Cup 2027

1 Oct – 13 Nov 2027, Australia. 24 teams (men's). Australia is 11 hours ahead (east coast) — evening Australian kick-off = morning local time. Final 13 Nov 2027, 20:00 AEDT = early morning local time — early morning pub possible.

Women's Rugby World Cup 2027

2027 co-hosted by Ireland, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. Games in the host countries are in local time and pub-friendly. If elsewhere, depends on timezone.

Rugby World Cup Sevens

Every four years. 2025 in Saudi Arabia — evening local time = early morning local time.

Sevens World Series

Series of 15-a-side sevens tournaments worldwide. October/November–May. Local Sevens stops are pub-friendly. Away stops in Europe are morning local time — pub-friendly if up early. Stops in Asia/A Americ|as are early morning local time.
