The Rugby Watch app serves Irish rugby fans — fixtures, kick-off times, and pub watchability for all Irish teams and tournaments, including European competition. This app is committed to GDPR compliance and your privacy.

What we collect

The Rugby Watch app does not collect personal data that identifies you. Specifically:

  • Group planning grid: any names or notes you add in the group planning section are stored only on your own device (using localStorage). We do not send this data to any server, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.
  • Advert identifiers: if you choose to accept non-essential adverts, the app uses your device's advertising identifier to serve adverts. This is standard for free apps. Your choice (accept or reject) is stored on your device.
  • App analytics (optional): if you enable analytics in the app settings, we may collect anonymous usage data (e.g. which screens are opened, how long the app is used, crash reports). This data does not identify you personally. You can turn this off in the app.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

Your choices

  • Adverts: before non-essential adverts load, the app asks whether you accept or reject them. You can change this choice at any time in the app. If you reject, only essential service adverts (where required by law) will appear.
  • Analytics: you can disable app analytics in the app settings at any time.
  • Advertising identifier:
    • On iOS: go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and turn off personalised ads. You can also reset your advertising identifier.
    • On Android: go to Settings > Google > Ads, and choose "Opt out of ad personalisation" or reset your advertising ID.
  • Delete your data: if you uninstall the app, all data stored on your device (group planning grid, consent choices, analytics preferences) is deleted.

GDPR — General Data Protection Regulation

This app is built to comply with the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) and ePrivacy rules. Here's what that means for you:

  • Lawful basis for processing: we process personal data only where we have a lawful basis. The group planning grid is stored on your device with your consent (localStorage). Advert identifiers are processed only if you accept non-essential adverts.
  • Consent: before non-essential adverts load, the app asks you to accept or reject them. Your choice is saved on your device using localStorage, and you can change it at any time in the app. If you reject, only essential service adverts (where required by law) will appear.
  • Data minimisation: we collect only what is strictly necessary. We do not collect your name, email, location, device contacts, photos, or any other personal data that identifies you. The planning grid stays on your device — we never send it to a server or cloud.
  • Your GDPR rights:
    • Right to withdraw consent: use the "Change advert preference" button in the app at any time.
    • Right to erasure: use the "Clear all" button to delete your planning data. Uninstalling the app deletes all localStorage data.
    • Right to access and portability: your data is stored only on your own device in localStorage — you can view it by inspecting your browser's localStorage at any time, and export it if needed.
    • Right to object/opt-out: on iOS, reset your advertising identifier in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising. On Android, opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads.
  • No cross-app tracking: we do not track you across other apps or websites. We do not set third-party tracking cookies.
  • Data retention: we retain your data only as long as you keep the app installed. Uninstalling deletes all stored data immediately.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

The only storage used is localStorage on your own device for the group planning grid and your advert consent choice.
