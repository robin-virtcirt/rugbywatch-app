The Rugby Watch app serves Irish rugby fans — fixtures, kick-off times, and pub watchability for all Irish teams and tournaments, including European competition.

What we collect

The Rugby Watch app does not collect personal data that identifies you. Specifically:

  • Group planning grid: any names or notes you add in the group planning section are stored only on your own device (using localStorage). We do not send this data to any server, and we cannot see it. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.
  • Advert identifiers: if you choose to accept non-essential adverts, the app uses your device's advertising identifier to serve adverts. This is standard for free apps. Your choice (accept or reject) is stored on your device.
  • App analytics (optional): if you enable analytics in the app settings, we may collect anonymous usage data (e.g. which screens are opened, how long the app is used, crash reports). This data does not identify you personally. You can turn this off in the app.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, device contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.

Your choices

  • Adverts: before non-essential adverts load, the app asks whether you accept or reject them. You can change this choice at any time in the app. If you reject, only essential service adverts (where required by law) will appear.
  • Analytics: you can disable app analytics in the app settings at any time.
  • Advertising identifier:
    • On iOS: go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and turn off personalised ads. You can also reset your advertising identifier.
    • On Android: go to Settings > Google > Ads, and choose "Opt out of ad personalisation" or reset your advertising ID.
  • Delete your data: if you uninstall the app, all data stored on your device (group planning grid, consent choices, analytics preferences) is deleted.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

Cookies and similar technologies

The Rugby Watch app does not use cookies in the traditional web-browser sense. The app may use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier) for ad delivery, as described above.
